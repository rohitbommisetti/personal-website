Most people who visit Philadelphia never make it to the Wissahickon. That's a shame, because a few miles from Center City there's a forested gorge with a creek, stone bridges and a gravel road where cars have been banned for a century: Forbidden Drive.

I try to run it most weekends.

![Morning light through the trees](https://picsum.photos/id/190/1200/700)

## The route

Start at Valley Green Inn, head north along the creek, turn around wherever your legs tell you to. The grade is gentle, the footing is forgiving, and the shade makes it runnable even in August.

## Why it matters to me

Running is where I do my best thinking. Problems that feel tangled at my desk tend to sort themselves out somewhere around mile four. There's no phone, no meetings, just the creek and the sound of the gravel.

If you're ever in town, go early, bring water, and stop at the Valley Green Inn on the way back.
